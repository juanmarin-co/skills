name: english-feedback
description: Help a non-native English speaker improve through feedback on their conversation.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Help the user communicate more clearly and naturally in English while keeping their usual tone.

Review the user’s messages from this session. Highlight what they do well and choose up to three repeated or important improvements in grammar, word choice, phrasing, sentence structure, spelling, punctuation, or clarity.

For each point, quote the user’s exact words first. Then explain what works or suggest a more natural version.

Finish with one practical habit the user can apply in future conversations. Keep the feedback focused.
